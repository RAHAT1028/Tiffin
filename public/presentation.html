⚠️ MARKET REALITY & PAIN POINTS

The School Lunch Crisis in Dhaka

Working parents and children face daily obstacles in maintaining wholesome nutrition.

⏰ 01. Morning Rush & Parental Stress
Parents wake up at 5:00 AM to cook before battling Dhaka morning traffic, leading to rushed, repetitive, and stressful meal preparation.
🍟 02. Unhealthy Canteen Junk Food
School canteens predominantly sell processed fried snacks, trans-fat singaras, and high-sugar sodas causing afternoon lethargy and poor focus.
❄️ 03. Cold, Soggy & Bacteria-Prone Lunches
Traditional plastic lunchboxes drop below 40°C in 2 hours, breeding food-borne bacteria and resulting in unappetizing meals.
🥜 04. Zero Institutional Allergen Controls
Lack of strict peanut, dairy, gluten, egg, and seafood segregation creates constant safety risks for sensitive children.
